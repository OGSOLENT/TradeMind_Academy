## What is SMT?

> **SMT Divergence — a structural divergence between correlated markets that should theoretically move together.**

Some markets are tightly correlated: the Nasdaq and the S&P 500, gold and silver. When they are behaving normally, both make higher highs together, or lower lows together.

**SMT divergence is when they stop confirming each other.** One makes a lower low; the other refuses. That "correlation crack" is evidence the move is not broadly supported — one market is being pushed to take liquidity while the other declines to follow. It often precedes a reversal.

This is why it is placed last in the course: it is a *confluence* tool, and confluence is only meaningful once you already have something to confirm.

## The Golden Rule

The most important rule in the lesson, stated three separate ways in the video:

> **I'm going to first find a model and then see if it has SMT for confluence.**

> **Never, ever traded in a vacuum.**

> **Always establish your framework first. Only then should you look for SMT divergence.**

The failure mode is going hunting for divergence across charts and treating anything you find as a signal. Correlated markets diverge constantly and most of it means nothing. It is only informative *at a location your framework already identified*.

### The workflow

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1. Wait for the model** | Ensure a valid model and proper candle closure (Modules 4–6) |
| **2. Check the asset** | Look at the correlated asset for structural divergence |
| **3. Execute** | Use the SMT as **secondary confluence** to enter |

Note "secondary" in step 3. SMT never promotes a bad setup into a good one; it strengthens a good one.

## Two types of SMT

| Feature | Reversal SMT | Continuation SMT |
|---|---|---|
| **Formation** | Structural turn | Trend continuation |
| **Candles** | C1 and C2 | C2 and C3 |
| **Action** | Can be messy | Cleaner expansion |

**Reversal SMT** appears at turning points, between Candle 1 and Candle 2. It catches the change of direction, but price action around a turn is often choppy.

**Continuation SMT** appears mid-trend, between Candle 2 and Candle 3. It confirms an existing move rather than calling a new one — and the expansion that follows is typically cleaner. For most traders it is the easier of the two.

## The Double SMT setup

Where both types appear in one session — the strongest configuration:

| Hour | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Hour 1** | Market opens and manipulates price low first |
| **Hour 2** | Forms a new protected intraday swing with **Double SMT** |
| **Hour 3** | Price breaks out and expands cleanly into daily targets |

Hour 1 is manipulation (Lesson 7's AMD). Hour 2 is where the protected swing forms *and* the divergence appears on both the reversal and continuation reads. Hour 3 delivers.

## Why SMT trades fail

An honest list, and worth studying more than the setup itself:

- **Price opens high instead of low in a long setup.** The manipulation went the wrong way; the premise is broken before the divergence matters.
- **Intraday action invalidates previous equilibrium.** The EQ invalidation from Lesson 15 overrides the SMT.
- **Hunted for SMT without a valid timeframe model.** The Golden Rule violated. The most common cause.
- **Reality check: nothing works 100% of the time.**

That last point is stated plainly in the video, and it belongs in every lesson. This is a probabilistic framework with defined invalidation, not a system of certainties.

## Key terms

- **SMT divergence** — correlated markets failing to confirm each other's highs or lows
- **Correlation crack** — the moment the divergence appears
- **Reversal SMT** — divergence at a turn (C1/C2)
- **Continuation SMT** — divergence mid-trend (C2/C3)
- **Double SMT** — both types present, forming a protected intraday swing
- **Secondary confluence** — a confirming factor, never a primary signal

## Common mistakes

- **Hunting for SMT first.** The Golden Rule exists because this is the default temptation.
- **Treating divergence as a signal on its own.** It is confluence.
- **Ignoring EQ invalidation** because the SMT looks compelling.
