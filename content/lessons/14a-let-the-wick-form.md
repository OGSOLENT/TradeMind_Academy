## Why do you keep getting stopped out early?

Not because the analysis was wrong. Because the *timing* was.

> **Timing costs capital.**

The bias can be correct, the level can be correct, and the trade still loses — because it was taken before the market had finished doing the one thing it always does first.

## Entering early makes you the liquidity

The sentence that should stay with you:

> **If you enter before the wick forms, YOU become the liquidity.**

| Timing | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Entering too early** | Makes you the liquidity |
| **Entering on time** | Catches the expansion |

Your stop, placed before the manipulation is complete, sits exactly where the market is heading to collect orders (Lesson 3). You are not unlucky — you are early, and early and wrong look identical on a statement.

## Defining the protected swing

> **Protected Swing — price sweeps a key level and closes through prior candles, shifting the trend.**

The three-part logic, all required:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1. Sweep level** | Price sweeps a high, low, or gap |
| **2. Reversal** | Short-term movement reverses direction |
| **3. Candle close** | Price closes through the prior candle series |

> **This logic confirms the swing is protected and ready for expansion.**

This is the same structure as Lesson 9's protected swing and Lesson 7's CISD, stated as an execution gate. Notice step 1 now explicitly includes a **gap** — the FVG from Lesson 7a is a valid thing to sweep into.

## The wick checklist

One question, asked before every entry:

> **Did the higher timeframe form its wick yet?**

- **YES** → proceed to execution.
- **NO** → wait. Do nothing.

That is the whole checklist, and it is binary on purpose. Most losing entries in this method fail this single gate.

## Trading the body

Once the wick is confirmed, execution is mechanical:

> **Let the wick form. Enter around the opening price. Trade the body.**

The candle's *body* is the expansion — the part that travels. The wick is the manipulation that precedes it. Entering near the opening price after the wick has formed puts you at the start of the body rather than inside the wick.

The three-step sequence:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1. Find swing** | Identify a confirmed protected swing |
| **2. Enter trade** | Enter near the higher-timeframe open |
| **3. Trade body** | Ride the expansion toward your target |

### Stop-loss placement

- **Stop on invalidation** — at the point that proves the idea wrong (Lesson 9), not a fixed distance
- **Wick holds** — the protected wick is the level that must not break
- **Aim 2R+** — the structure should offer at least twice the risk

Entry sits near the open, the stop sits beyond the wick, and the target is measured in R multiples of that distance.

> *Position sizing — turning "2R" into an actual order quantity — is Module 3.*

## Multi-timeframe: fractal market logic

The nesting from Lesson 5, applied to wick formation:

**Daily → 4-Hour → 1-Hour → 15-Minute → 5-Minute**

The higher timeframe supplies the wick that must form; the lower timeframe supplies the precision:

> **A daily wick formation can be pinpointed using a 5-minute swing.**

You are not choosing between timeframes. The daily tells you *what* has to happen; the 5-minute tells you the exact moment it has happened. The confirmed protected wick on the low timeframe is your entry trigger for the high-timeframe idea.

## Key terms

- **Becoming the liquidity** — entering before the manipulation completes, so your stop feeds it
- **Protected swing** — sweep → reversal → close through the prior series
- **The wick checklist** — has the higher timeframe formed its wick, yes or no
- **Trading the body** — entering for the expansion, not inside the manipulation
- **2R** — a target of at least twice the risked distance

## Common mistakes

- **Entering on the level rather than after the wick.** The level is where the wick forms, not where the move starts.
- **Placing the stop inside the wick.** It must sit beyond the protected wick, at invalidation.
- **Answering the wick checklist with "nearly".** It is a yes/no gate.
