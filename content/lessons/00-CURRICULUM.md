## The ordering problem

The 29 videos were not recorded in teaching order. Several later videos silently assume ideas defined in others — for example, *Candle 2 Closures* tells the learner to "wait for a change in state of delivery" 12 minutes before *Mastering the CISD* ever defines what that is.

The order below is built from the **actual dependency graph** of the concepts, so that no lesson uses a term that a previous lesson has not already defined. It resolves every forward reference except the two listed under Gaps at the end.

| # | Module | Lessons | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|

| 6 | Daily Bias | 5 | Which direction am I trading today, and when do I enter? |
| 7 | The Fractal Model | 4 | What is the complete system, and where do I take profit? |
| 8 | SMT Divergence | 1 | How do I add confluence without fooling myself? |
| 9 | Weekly Profiles | 5 | What shape is this week taking, and when do I act? |

**Why Risk & Position Sizing sits at module 3.** Every module from 5 onward quotes an "R multiple" — 2R targets, "risk 1 unit to make 2.55". Those are meaningless until sizing is taught, and a learner should understand what they are risking before they learn entry patterns. Placing it third resolves those forward references.

## Gaps — recommended videos still to make

These concepts are **used repeatedly but never taught** in the current 25. A beginner following the course in order will hit each of them as an undefined term.

| Priority | Missing lesson | Where it's assumed |
|---|---|---|
| ~~High~~ | ~~Fair Value Gaps~~ | ✅ **Covered** by `How_Fair_Value_Gaps_Work.mp4` (Lesson 7a) |
| ~~High~~ | ~~Risk management & position sizing~~ | ✅ **Covered** by the two Module 3 videos |
| **High** | **Kill zones & session timing** — the London/New York windows as a filter | Lesson 13's execution checklist opens with "Kill Zone"; Lesson 16 automates it; Lesson 14's session matrix touches it but never teaches the windows |
| **High** | **Points of Interest — the family** — one lesson naming order block, FVG, breaker, liquidity pool and when each applies | Used as "POI" from Lesson 5 onward as if already defined |
| Medium | **Backtesting method** — how to validate the model on historical data | Nothing covers it, and for a dissertation artefact it is the natural bridge from "here is a model" to "here is evidence it works" |
| Medium | **Trade journaling & review** — logging setups, grading execution | Implied by every "checklist" but never taught |
| Medium | **Trading psychology** — patience, the impatient-trader trap, tilt after a loss | Lessons 12, 14 and 14a all raise it, then move on |
| Low | **Market structure vocabulary** — higher highs/lows, break of structure, CHoCH | `CHoCH` and `BOS` appear on a Lesson 25 diagram with no definition |
| Low | **Breaker & mitigation blocks** | Adjacent to order blocks (Lesson 11); referenced in the wider method but not here |
| ~~Low~~ | ~~Chart & platform setup~~ | ✅ Largely covered by `Perfect_Position_Sizing_in_TradingView.mp4` |

Making **Kill Zones** and a **Points of Interest** overview would close every remaining forward reference in the course. **Backtesting** is the highest-value addition beyond that, because it is what turns the model into evidence.
