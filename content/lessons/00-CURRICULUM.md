# TradeMind Academy — Level 1 Curriculum

**Source:** 25 unique video lessons in `/Lessons` (~2.9 hours total).
**Written from:** the on-screen content of every video (slides extracted and read frame-by-frame).
**Status:** written lessons complete for all 25 existing videos. Gaps for future videos listed at the end.

> **Educational content only.** Every chart and price used in these lessons is illustrative or simulated. Nothing here is financial advice, a signal, or a recommendation to trade. For adults 18+.

**Attribution.** The videos credit **mmxm Trader** for many of the daily-bias and next-day-model concepts, and the fractal model is presented as the **TTrades Fractal Model (TTFM)**. Keep these credits in the final platform copy.

---

## The ordering problem

The 25 videos were not recorded in teaching order. Several later videos silently assume ideas defined in others — for example, *Candle 2 Closures* tells the learner to "wait for a change in state of delivery" 12 minutes before *Mastering the CISD* ever defines what that is.

The order below is built from the **actual dependency graph** of the concepts, so that no lesson uses a term that a previous lesson has not already defined. It resolves every forward reference except one (noted in Module 8).

## Gaps — recommended videos still to make

These concepts are **used repeatedly but never taught** in the current 25. A beginner following the course in order will hit each of them as an undefined term.

| Priority | Missing lesson | Where it's assumed |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)** — what they are, how to mark them | Lessons 7, 9, 10, 13 all use FVGs as a point of interest with no definition |
| **High** | **Risk management & position sizing** — the 1-unit risk model, R multiples | Lessons 11, 13, 18 quote "2.55R" and "risk 1 unit" but never teach sizing |
| **High** | **Kill zones & session timing** — London/NY windows as a filter | Lesson 13's execution checklist opens with "Kill Zone"; Lesson 16 automates it |
| Medium | **Points of Interest (POI)** — a single lesson defining the family (order block, FVG, breaker) | Used from Lesson 5 onward as if already known |
| Medium | **Trade journaling & review** — logging setups, grading execution | Implied by "master checklist" but never taught |
| Medium | **Trading psychology** — patience, the impatient-trader trap | Lesson 12 and 14 both raise it ("trust emotions or the mechanics?") then move on |
| Low | **Chart setup & tools** — platform, timeframes, Fibonacci settings for projections | Lesson 17 assumes the Fib tool is already configured |

Making **FVG, Risk Management and Kill Zones** would close every remaining forward reference in the course.
