## Two trades, one model

The lessons before this one gave you the parts. This one runs them as two whole trades, one reversal and one continuation, on the hourly and 5-minute pair with the 4-hour giving bias. Both walkthroughs step through the same charts I describe here, so read the text once and then click through the steps with the chart in front of you.

## Trade one: the reversal

**Bias.** The 4-hour chart has printed a Candle 2 low overnight: it swept the previous 4-hour low and closed back above it. That's a bullish 4-hour count, and it points the hourly model up for the session.

**Count.** It's 08:00 in New York. The 07:00 hourly candle made a low that sits on the 4-hour's swing. The 08:00 candle opens and dips below the 07:00 low. If it closes back above, it's an hourly Candle 2 with the 4-hour bias behind it.

**T-Spot.** The expected wick zone for the 08:00 candle is the band between the 07:00 low and the 4-hour's equilibrium underneath it. That's where the wick should form if this is the reversal.

**CISD.** On the 5-minute, at 08:20, price has run below the 07:00 low. At 08:30 a 5-minute candle closes above the open of the run down. Delivery has changed; a protected low exists at the 08:20 low.

**Trigger.** On the 1-minute, the run down left a bearish fair value gap at 08:17. At 08:31 a 1-minute candle closes above it. Inversion. The entry is that close.

**Stop.** Just under the 1-minute low the inversion formed from, at 08:29. A handful of points.

**Exit.** The 08:00 candle's profile so far is small early wick, body building: expansion. So the exit is the projected close: the first standard deviation projection from the 4-hour's Candle 2 swing (Lesson 17), which sits above the 07:00 high. If the profile had been a big wick, the exit would have been the daily open instead.

**Result.** The hourly candle expands through the 07:00 high and closes near it. The projection is hit at 08:52. The 08:00 candle closes as an expansion candle, which is what the profile said it would be.

## Trade two: the continuation

**Count.** Same session, later. The 08:00 candle was Candle 2. The 09:00 candle closed above the 08:00 high: Candle 3, direction confirmed. It's 10:00, and the 10:00 candle is Candle 4.

**The open.** The 10:00 candle opens in the upper half of the 09:00 candle's range. The equilibrium rule is satisfied.

**The dip.** On the 5-minute, from 10:00 to 10:15 price pulls back. It stops at the 5-minute order block left by the 09:00 expansion, which sits above the 09:00 candle's midpoint. That's the ideal refinement level from Lesson 53.

**IC-CISD.** At 10:20 a 5-minute candle closes above the open of the 10:00 to 10:15 run down. The 10:00 hourly candle is still open, but its lower-timeframe delivery has turned back up.

**Entry** on the close at 10:20. **Stop** under the 10:15 low. **Target** the 09:00 high first, then the next projection.

**Result.** The 09:00 high is taken at 10:35 and the projection at 10:50, still inside the 10:00 candle and still inside the Silver Bullet window (Lesson 38). The 10:00 candle closes as a Candle 4 continuation, expanding out of the upper half of Candle 3, exactly as the count expects.

## The checklist

Every TTFM trade passes this before the entry, or it isn't a TTFM trade.

| Check | Reversal | Continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Bias from one pair up | 4-hour Candle 2 or 3 in my direction | Same |
| Count on my pair | Hourly forming Candle 2 | Hourly Candle 3 closed; this is Candle 4 |
| Location | Price in the T-Spot | Candle 4 opened in Candle 3's upper half; dip holds above the midpoint |
| Confirmation | 5-minute CISD, protected swing formed | 5-minute IC-CISD off the order block or equilibrium |
| Trigger | 1-minute inversion (optional refinement) | The IC-CISD close, or its retest |
| Stop | Under the inversion low | Under the protected low |
| Exit, decided now | 2R, next hourly open, or the projection, by the profile | Candle 3's high, then the projection |
| Timing | Not in the last quarter of the hourly candle | Same |

Eight rows. If any row is blank, you're guessing, and the model doesn't guess.

## Grading the trade

After the exit, whatever it was, grade the trade on the checklist, not the result (Lesson 47).

- **A.** Every row filled before entry, followed to the exit.
- **B.** Every row filled, but the exit was changed mid-trade.
- **C.** A row was missing or bent to make the trade fit.

A losing A is a good trade. A winning C is a bad one, and it's the more dangerous of the two, because it teaches you to bend rows. Twenty A trades in the journal, win or lose, is the point where you know the model is yours.

## Key terms

- **Reversal trade**: catching Candle 2 in the T-Spot with a CISD and, optionally, a 1-minute inversion
- **Continuation trade**: joining Candle 4 with an IC-CISD off the refinement level
- **The checklist**: the eight rows every trade fills before the entry
- **Trade grade**: A, B or C for execution, independent of the result

## Common mistakes

- **Running the reversal without the 4-hour bias.** An hourly Candle 2 against the 4-hour is a retracement of the 4-hour.
- **Taking the continuation from below Candle 3's midpoint.** The count is already breaking.
- **Changing the exit mid-trade.** That's a B at best.
- **Grading on the result.** The result is noise; the checklist is the signal.
