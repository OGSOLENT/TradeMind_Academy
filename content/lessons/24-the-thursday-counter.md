## The profile

> **Thursday Counter — a weekly profile where Thursday completely reverses the established 3-day directional range.**

Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday all move one way. By Thursday the direction looks settled, the trend looks confirmed, and it is the safest-looking moment of the week to join.

Then Thursday reverses the whole thing.

This is the most psychologically difficult profile in the module, because the trap is set precisely where confidence peaks.

## Defining the trap

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Mon–Wed** | Price expands away from the weekly open |
| **No significant low/high** | No meaningful counter-swing is created |
| **Thursday** | Forms a candle countering the range |

The middle row is the tell. A healthy three-day trend leaves **protected swings** behind it (Lesson 9) — pullbacks that hold, structure that builds. A trend that produces *no significant counter-swing* has no structure supporting it. It is a one-way run, and one-way runs invite a counter.

**Who is caught:** retail participants who joined the trend on Wednesday and Thursday, positioned at the extreme with no protective structure beneath them. Thursday's reversal at the **16:00 EST close** takes exactly that liquidity.

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Expansion begins |
| **Tuesday** | Expansion continues |
| **Wednesday** | Final exhausted expansion |
| **Thursday** | The counter reversal — the "reversal loop" |
| **Friday** | Continuation to targets |

"Final exhausted expansion" on Wednesday is the same exhaustion signature you learned to read on a single candle in Lesson 4: the move consuming its remaining budget. Here it happens across three days and shows up as a **topping-out** pattern.

## Reversal mechanics — the hourly trigger

> **When are we going to want to look inside this candle for an hourly change?**

Once Thursday prints a candle countering the range, drop into it on the hourly and look for the **Change in Delivery State**. Same two-tier structure as every profile: daily signal, hourly proof.

The sequence: **daily candle counters the range → drop to hourly → Ch-DoS confirms → trade Friday's continuation.**

## The three confirming indicators

| Indicator | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **SMT Divergence** | Sweeps a previous high/low while the correlated asset does not |
| **Daily wick** | A small wick, with SMT present |
| **State of delivery** | An hourly chart shift |

Read together as a chain: **daily sweep → daily wick rejection → hourly structure shift.** Each confirms the one before it. SMT (Lesson 20) is doing its proper job here as secondary confluence on a setup the framework already identified.

Note the **small wick** requirement — Lesson 4's rule persisting to the end of the course. A small wick on the countering candle means the extreme formed early and expansion is still available for Friday.

## Friday targets

Unlike the classic expansion week (Lesson 22), where Friday is a day to avoid, **the Thursday counter makes Friday the delivery day**. Thursday sets up; Friday delivers, hitting the **final open objective**.

The difference is where the week's opportunity sits. In a classic expansion week the move happened Wednesday and Thursday, so Friday is spent. In a Thursday counter the reversal only began Thursday, so Friday carries the expansion.

This is why profile recognition matters: the *same day* is a no-trade day in one shape and the main event in another.

## When setups fail

Not every Thursday reversal is this profile. The distinguishing features:

- The three-day range must be genuinely **established** — a real directional run
- The counter must **completely reverse** it, not merely pull back
- The **hourly change in delivery state** must confirm

A Thursday pullback with no hourly confirmation is a pullback. Absent these, you are looking at ordinary noise late in the week.

## Key terms

- **Thursday counter** — Thursday reversing an established three-day range
- **Reversal loop** — the counter-move pattern on Thursday
- **Topping out** — the exhaustion signature of the final expansion day
- **Ch-DoS** — change in delivery state, the hourly confirmation
- **Final open objective** — Friday's target

## Common mistakes

- **Joining the trend on Wednesday.** The exhausted third day is where the trap is baited.
- **Trading Thursday's daily candle without the hourly confirmation.**
- **Applying Lesson 22's "avoid Friday" rule here.** In this profile Friday is the delivery day.
