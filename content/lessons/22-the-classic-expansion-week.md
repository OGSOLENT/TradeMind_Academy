## The profile

> **Classic Expansion — a directional weekly candle with the high/low early in the week, followed by expansion and Friday capping.**

This is the most common and cleanest weekly shape, and the counterpart to Lesson 21's mid-week reversal. Here the weekly extreme forms **early**, and the rest of the week travels in one direction.

| Scenario | What forms early |
|---|---|
| **Bearish** | Monday or Tuesday forms the **high** |
| **Bullish** | Monday or Tuesday forms the **low** |

## Setting the weekly bias

The goal is to anticipate the move before it starts, and the framing is exact:

> **The whole goal is to let the low of the week form and then look to trade.**

You are not trying to catch Monday's move. You are waiting for Monday or Tuesday to *finish making the extreme*, then trading the expansion away from it. This is the daily wick logic (Lesson 14) scaled up to the week: Monday and Tuesday build the weekly wick.

## Forming the high or low — Monday and Tuesday patience

Monday and Tuesday are **observation days**. The market is forming the extreme; you are letting it.

The signal to watch for:

> **Look for a strong Candle 2 closure indicating a shift toward your bias.**

That is Lesson 5's pattern again, at the daily level, inside the first two days of the week. When it prints, the weekly extreme is likely set.

## Confirming the setup — validating the wick

Three steps:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1. Daily closure** | Wait for a Daily Candle 2 closure |
| **2. Hourly chart** | Drop down to the hourly timeframe |
| **3. Delivery shift** | Check for a Change in State of Delivery |

The same top-down structure as everywhere else: daily signal, hourly proof.

### The hourly checks

The hourly chart does three specific jobs:

- **Verifies the wick** is complete
- **Closes opposing candles** — the CISD
- **Confirms safe trading** — that the extreme is genuinely in

## Trading the expansion

Once confirmed, the trading days are Wednesday and Thursday:

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **Mon/Tue** | Observe |
| **Wed** | Trade |
| **Thu** | Trade |

> **Wednesday is primed for aggressive price movement in the direction of your bias.**

Wednesday delivers the main expansion — Candle 3 in the four-candle sequence from Lesson 5. Thursday is the secondary continuation, Candle 4:

> **The fractal model anticipates a secondary expansion to reach higher-timeframe objectives.**

### The full week

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Mon–Tue** | Retrace, oppose the bias, form the wick and confirm |
| **Wed** | Aggressive expansion — Candle 3 |
| **Thu** | Secondary continuation — Candle 4 |
| **Fri** | Cap the range with a TGIF retracement — avoid trading |

The four-candle sequence you learned in Module 3 maps directly onto the trading week. Monday/Tuesday are the reference and the sweep; Wednesday and Thursday are the continuation and follow-through.

## When to walk away

Friday has its own rule, and it is a discipline lesson as much as a technical one.

**Avoid trading Friday** because:

- **The expansion is complete** — the week's move has been delivered
- **Targets are hit** — the objectives you set have been reached
- **TGIF retracement** — Friday typically retraces part of the weekly range rather than extending it

Recognising that the week's opportunity is finished, and stopping, is a skill. Most Friday trades are attempts to extract more from a week that has already given what it had.

## Predicting, or reacting?

The closing question of the lesson, and of the method. The classic expansion week is not a forecast that Monday will make the low. It is a framework that says: *if* Monday or Tuesday makes the extreme, *and* a Candle 2 closure confirms it, *and* the hourly shows a delivery shift — then Wednesday and Thursday are tradeable. Each step is a reaction to something the market has already done.

## Key terms

- **Classic expansion** — weekly extreme forms early, expansion follows
- **Observation days** — Monday and Tuesday, when the wick forms
- **Secondary expansion** — Thursday's continuation, Candle 4
- **TGIF retracement** — Friday's tendency to pull back inside the weekly range
- **Capping** — Friday closing out the weekly range rather than extending it

## Common mistakes

- **Trading Monday's move.** Monday is building the weekly wick you intend to trade away from.
- **Trading Friday.** The expansion is complete and targets are usually hit.
- **Skipping the hourly delivery shift** because the daily closure looked strong.
