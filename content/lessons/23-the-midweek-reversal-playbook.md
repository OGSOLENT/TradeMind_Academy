## The profile

Lesson 21 introduced the intraweek reversal; this lesson is the tradeable playbook for it, including the messy versions.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1. Opposing bias** | Monday and Tuesday go **opposite** to the weekly bias |
| **2. Wednesday reversal** | Wednesday forms a reversal candle |
| **3. Thurs/Fri expansion** | Trade the intended direction expansion |

The defining feature is step 1: the first two days move *against* the direction the week will ultimately take. That opening move is a trap — it builds the liquidity that fuels the real move.

Where the classic expansion week (Lesson 22) sets its extreme early and runs, the midweek reversal sets a *false* extreme early and reverses.

## The perfect setup

> **Candle 2 Closure — a Wednesday reversal candle aligning with higher-timeframe bias.**

When this prints, **the weekly wick is set**. Monday and Tuesday built it; Wednesday's closure confirms it is finished.

Two conditions:

1. A **Candle 2 closure** on Wednesday
2. **Alignment with the higher-timeframe bias** — the reversal goes the way the weekly/monthly picture already pointed

A Wednesday reversal *against* the higher-timeframe bias is not this setup.

## The hourly trigger

> **Hourly State Change — a directional shift on the 1-hour lower timeframe confirming the reversal.**

The daily reversal candle is the signal; the hourly state change is the proof. Same two-tier structure as every other lesson.

### The perfect week, hour by hour

| Day | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Expands higher | False move |
| **Tuesday** | Forms a reversal | Reversal confirmed |
| **Wednesday** | Drops to the POI and closes | Hourly check |
| **Thurs/Fri** | Hourly confirms, price expands | **Green light** — trade |

The green light comes only after the hourly check on Wednesday. Everything before that is observation.

## When Wednesday isn't clean

> **What if Wednesday's reversal isn't clean?**

Often it isn't. The adaptation:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1. Range sweep** | Identify the range sweep on Wednesday |
| **2. Hourly close** | Wait for an hourly close over the key level |
| **3. Trade expansion** | Trade the expansion Thursday/Friday |

Instead of demanding a textbook daily Candle 2 closure, you accept a **range sweep** plus an **hourly close over a key level**. The evidence bar shifts from the daily to the hourly — lower resolution on the signal, same requirement for proof.

The general approach to messy charts: **zoom in to the 1-hour, and let the calendar day close give you the answer.**

## Rules and exceptions

The lesson's most useful table — three scenarios, three evidence standards:

| Scenario | Setup | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| **Perfect** | Candle 2 | Hourly CSD |
| **Sweep** | Inside bar | Hourly close |
| **Messy** | Imperfect | Thursday close |

**Perfect** — a clean daily Candle 2 with an hourly change in state of delivery. Gets you in early with the best price.

**Sweep** — Wednesday produces an inside bar rather than a clean closure. Wait for an hourly close over the key level.

**Messy** — nothing is clean. Wait for the **Thursday close**. You enter late and with a worse price, but you enter on evidence rather than hope.

The principle: **as the setup degrades, the evidence requirement rises.** You do not lower your standard to keep trading a week that is not cooperating — you raise the bar and accept a later entry.

## Beyond the template

The profiles in this module are patterns that recur, not laws. Their value is in telling you *when to look* and *what evidence to demand*. A week that fits none of them is a week to sit out — which the "messy" row implicitly allows, since a Thursday close that never comes means no trade at all.

## Key terms

- **Midweek reversal** — Monday/Tuesday move against the weekly bias; Wednesday reverses
- **Weekly wick** — the false move built by the first two days
- **Hourly state change** — the 1-hour directional shift confirming the reversal
- **Range sweep** — a sweep of the established range in place of a clean closure
- **Inside bar** — a day taking neither the prior high nor low

## Common mistakes

- **Trading Monday's move** as if it reflects the week's direction. It is the trap.
- **Demanding a perfect Candle 2** and missing the sweep and messy variations.
- **Lowering the evidence bar on a messy week.** It should go up, not down.
