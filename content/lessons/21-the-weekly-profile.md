## What if the weekly high isn't set early?

Most weeks establish their extreme early — Monday or Tuesday — and expand from there (Lesson 22). But some do not. When the high or low forms **mid-week**, the shape of the whole week changes, and so does when you should be trading.

Module 8 is about recognising which shape you are in. It is the timing layer that sits on top of the model.

## The intraweek reversal

> **Intraweek reversal — a profile where the high or low forms mid-week.**

The day-by-day anatomy:

| Day | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Expands and sets the initial tone |
| **Tuesday** | Consolidates with a smaller range |
| **Wednesday** | Forms the actual reversal pivot |
| **Thurs/Fri** | High-probability continuations |

Monday sets a tone that turns out to be the manipulation. Tuesday's smaller range is the pause. **Wednesday is the pivot** — the day the real weekly extreme forms. Thursday and Friday deliver the expansion in the true direction.

Recognising this shape by Tuesday's close means you are ready for Wednesday rather than trapped by Monday.

## Use the logic, not the pattern

> **You want to actually use the logic and combine it with a daily and hourly fractal model.**

The weekly profile is *context*, not a trading system. It tells you which day to expect the pivot. Your entry still comes from the daily and hourly model in Modules 4–6: the daily gives bias, the hourly confirms, the protected swing executes.

Trading "it's Wednesday, so I'll buy" is exactly the pattern trading this course has warned against since Lesson 5.

## The checklist of engagement

To validate a mid-week reversal:

> **State of Delivery — an hourly shift confirming a daily reversal candle before trading the expansion.**

Two levels of evidence, both required:

1. A **daily reversal candle** — the higher-timeframe signal
2. An **hourly shift** (CISD) confirming it — the lower-timeframe proof

### What counts, and what doesn't

- **A Candle 2 closure strongly signals a reversal.** The sweep-and-close-back-in on the daily is your evidence.
- **Consolidation is *not* a reversal signature.** Sideways price is indecision, not a turn. A tight range on Wednesday means the reversal has not happened — it does not mean it is happening quietly.

This distinction catches many traders in the middle of the week: a quiet Wednesday feels like a pivot forming. It is not evidence.

## The classic setup

The picture-perfect mid-week reversal:

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Aggressive move out of the POI |
| **Tuesday** | Small consolidation |
| **Wednesday** | Manipulates the low and forms the closure |
| **Thurs/Fri** | Continuation expansions — "beautiful continuation" |

And the rule that governs it:

> **The hourly checkpoint is non-negotiable for trade confirmation.**

No hourly confirmation, no trade. The weekly shape does not override the model.

## Messy action

Real weeks are often not clean. One reliable discriminator:

- **An Open-High-Low-Close (OHLC) candle is a false reversal.**
- **An Open-Low-High-Close (OLHC) candle forms a true reversal.**

This is Lesson 13's sequence logic used as a filter. On a bullish reversal day you want the **low made early** (OLHC) — that leaves the rest of the day for expansion upward. If the high comes first (OHLC), the day is bearish in structure no matter what the close looks like.

A messy week might run: Monday expands away from the opening → Wednesday fails to form a true reversal → Thursday forms the true bullish reversal → Friday continuation. The pivot slid by a day; the logic did not change.

## SMT divergence in the week

> **SMT Divergence — a correlated asset reaches a gap, validating a reversal when the primary doesn't.**

If your market's mid-week reversal is ambiguous but the correlated market delivers a clean one, that divergence validates the turn (Lesson 20).

### Advanced confluences

Stack these for the strongest mid-week reversals:

- Monthly timeframe shows a candle closure
- Price respects 50% of the wick
- Targets clean equal lows
- The range high has been taken out
- SMT divergence confirms

## Key terms

- **Weekly profile** — the characteristic shape a week takes
- **Intraweek reversal** — the weekly extreme forming mid-week
- **Checklist of engagement** — daily reversal candle plus hourly shift
- **OHLC vs OLHC** — false vs true reversal sequences
- **Hourly checkpoint** — the non-negotiable confirmation step

## Common mistakes

- **Trading the day of the week instead of the model.**
- **Reading consolidation as a reversal.** It is indecision.
- **Skipping the hourly checkpoint** because the weekly shape looks textbook.
