Mean Reversion Dynamics

Analyzing standard deviation bands to identify overextended price action before the elastic snap-back.

When an asset's price moves dramatically away from its historical mean, mathematical probability suggests an eventual return to that average. This is the core principle of mean reversion.

However, executing trades purely on deviation is hazardous. We utilize Bollinger Bands alongside volume profile to distinguish between a genuine trend breakdown and a temporary statistical anomaly.
